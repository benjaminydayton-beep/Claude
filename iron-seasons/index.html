    Iron Seasons

    The legislature has granted you a railroad charter across an unsettled territory. You have 30 years. The towns out here are fragile: the ones you serve will grow, and the ones you ignore will empty out. The land fights back every season.

    • Towns live on what you haul. Grain feeds them, building supplies and steel make them grow, and coal keeps them alive through winter. A town that gets no passenger service shrinks, and its people move to towns that do.
    • Industries follow your rails. Mines and farms you serve well will expand. Ones you ignore for years shut down. New ones open near your track.
    • Weather is a bet. Spring floods wash out light track on floodplains and bridges. Winter snow closes hill and mountain track unless it's engineered with snow sheds or tunnels. Read the Almanac's forecast and decide whether to pay for crews.
    • Boomtowns appear after silver strikes in the hills. Connect one within a year or it goes bust.

    How to start: put a Station next to two towns, survey Track between them (click the start, then the end, then Build), and order a Train that stops at both. Space pauses. Keys 1–4 set the speed.
