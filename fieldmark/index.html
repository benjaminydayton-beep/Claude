Getting a good photo

Focus. Keep the reticle on one bird until the ring closes and turns green. Moving the camera resets it, so flying birds are harder.

Light. Field marks vanish in the dark. Dawn and dusk are warm and bright enough; at night use the headlamp, lanterns, the campfire or the lighthouse beam.

Stealth. Walking is noisy. A yellow ? means a bird is uneasy and a red ! means it is about to flush. Sneak, or stand still and let birds come to you.

Timing. Each species keeps its own hours. Owls come out at dusk, and dawn brings the busiest chorus. Listen for calls; floating notes show who is singing.
